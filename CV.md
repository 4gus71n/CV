## Summary

What I like about mobile development is that I’ve seen it evolve from the very beginning. I remember playing with the J2ME framework on my Nokia 5300 back in high school—long before Android was a thing. The first time I saw a smartphone, I instantly knew that I wanted to pursue a career in mobile development.

I'm a Mobile Engineer with over a decade of experience in Android and iOS development. From the early days of Android 2.2 and ActionBarSherlock to modern architectures like MVVM with Jetpack Compose, I've built and led mobile teams across startups and large enterprises. I’m passionate about scalable architecture, remote collaboration, and mentoring engineers to reach their potential.
